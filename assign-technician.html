Assign technician

Assign an existing PowerGrid incident to a field technician. The incident remains the single source of truth.

Assignment details

Shared workflow

  1. Assignment is stored on the incident.
  2. The assigned technician accepts it in Technician Dashboard.
  3. Technician progress updates this same incident.
  4. Repair completion enters resident confirmation.

Technicians cannot change priority, take over another job, or directly reassign an incident.
